## CPU TjMax reference data

- The bundled `cpu-tjmax.json` lookup contains maximum-temperature values
  compiled from publicly published vendor specifications:
  - Intel product specifications: https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/products/details/processors.html
  - Intel temperature support article: https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000005597/processors.html
  - AMD product specifications: https://www.amd.com/en/products/processors.html
- This data is provided for informational purposes; always consult the official
  vendor documentation for your exact processor model.
